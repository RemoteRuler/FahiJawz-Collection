## 🌟 Overview & Key Highlights

This portal serves as the single source of truth for all operational, sales, and legal guidelines governing **FahiJawz Collection**:

- 📜 **Complete Bilingual Guidelines:** Every policy is clearly drafted with authentic Bengali (`বাংলা`) statements and accurate English translations.
- 🔍 **Interactive Live Search:** Instant client-side search and filtering across all clauses and terms.
- 🌐 **Language Filtering Modes:** Toggle smoothly between **Both Languages**, **বাংলা Only**, or **English Only**.
- 📋 **Executive At-a-Glance Summary:** 4 high-priority highlight cards summarizing delivery windows, mandatory parcel inspection, no-return policy, and customer hygiene protection.
- 🏛️ **Ultra-Luxury Marble & Imperial Gold Aesthetics:** Custom ivory-cream marble palette, concentric gold rim medallion, Roman serif typography, and elegant botanical flourishes.

## 📑 Core Policy Sections

1. **Data Privacy Policy (তথ্য গোপনীয়তা নীতি)**
   - Strict limitation to order delivery data (Name, Contact Number, Full Address).
   - Absolute confidentiality; zero third-party disclosure except licensed courier partners.
2. **Intellectual Property & Copyright (মেধাস্বত্ব ও সর্বস্বত্ব সংরক্ষণ)**
   - Protection of all original photography, videography, bespoke motifs, and brand descriptions.
   - Strict prohibition of unauthorized downloading, cloning, or commercial exploitation.
3. **Handcrafted Product Disclaimer (হস্তশিল্প পণ্যের বিশেষত্ব)**
   - Recognition of authentic handcrafted Jamdani and Crochet craftsmanship.
   - Natural artisanal variations in weave, texture, and hue as genuine marks of craft.
4. **Terms & Conditions of Sale (বিক্রয় ও ডেলিভারি নীতিমালা)**
   - Delivery windows: 2–4 days (Mymensingh) and 3–5 days (Nationwide Bangladesh).
   - Strict no-booking, no-return, no-refund, no-exchange policy; mandatory inspection with courier.
   - Prohibition of dress trials upon delivery to uphold strict customer hygiene.
   - Size chart review requirements and restriction against speculative multi-ordering.
5. **General Conditions (সাধারণ ব্যবসায়িক শর্তাবলী)**
   - Quality control benchmarks and inventory cancellation rights.
   - Display calibration disclaimer across diverse electronic screens.
   - Objective standards of inspection upon delivery.
6. **Official Inquiries & Customer Care (গ্রাহক সেবা ও যোগাযোগ)**
   - Direct verified links to official Facebook Page and Messenger support channels.
